# Landing page inspiration

- [Vapi](https://vapi.ai/) puts a working call interaction close to its developer-focused hero. WadaPakka brings its existing L1 and L2 product into the page instead of using a product mockup.
- [Retell AI](https://www.retellai.com/) groups product capabilities around a real call journey. The WadaPakka page moves from failure cases to verification steps, demo, and measured outcomes.
- [Deepgram](https://deepgram.com/) makes the underlying speech stack legible to builders. The hero names Prisma and Timbre, and the architecture section shows where the reliability layer sits.
- [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/) uses a short, high-contrast hero with one primary action. The WadaPakka hero keeps a clear promise and a direct link to the live demo.
- [Linear](https://linear.app/) gives product content generous space and clear type hierarchy. The problem, process, and results sections use that editorial pacing rather than stacking generic cards.
- [21st.dev](https://21st.dev/) and [React Bits](https://reactbits.dev/) show how a single expressive background can carry a hero. The existing GradientASCII supplies that motion here; FolderFloat remains a demo switcher.
- [The Component Gallery](https://component.gallery/) documents accessible tab patterns. The FolderFloat switcher is paired with keyboard-operable tabs and direct buttons for Eval, Ledger, and Handoff.

The page borrows layout and interaction patterns, not source code, logos, or copy. [opensourceui.in](https://www.opensourceui.in/) was also checked but was unavailable during this pass.
