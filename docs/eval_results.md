# Evaluation results — 10 November 2026

## Text scenarios (`python eval.py`, also `GET /api/eval`)

Eight hand-authored synthetic cases: naive **1/8 (12.5%)**, reliability layer **8/8 (100%)** exact amount/date/mode triples. The two paths receive *different supplied transcripts* in seven cases. These figures demonstrate the intended failure scenarios and the normalizer on known text; they do **not** measure Prisma accuracy or causal improvement from the layer. A layer hit assumes the borrower would confirm the read back. The application itself never silently commits the layer's first guess.

## Live audio (`python eval.py --audio`)

Three synthetic lines were generated by Timbre v2.5 (`Poorvi`, `hi-en`) as 16 kHz WAV and sent to Prisma v3.0 (`hi-IN,en-IN`, `transcribe`) on 10 November 2026. The generated files are in [`audio/`](audio/). The last run scored **2/3 (66.7%)** exact amount/date/mode triples for both parsers *before borrower confirmation*. The layer made **0 unconfirmed writes**. No borrower confirmation was simulated or counted as a corrected STT error.

| Spoken synthetic line | Prisma transcript | Gold triple | Parsed triple | Exact? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `parso dhai hazaar ... UPI` | `मैं परसों 2,500 रुपये UPI से दे दूंगा।` | ₹2,500 / 2026-11-12 / UPI | Same | Yes |
| `parso sawa teen sau ... cash` | `मैं परसों 3500 रुपये कैश से दे दूंगा।` | ₹325 / 2026-11-12 / CASH | ₹3,500 / same date / CASH | No |
| `agle somvaar do hazaar ... bank transfer` | `मैं अगले सोमवार 2000 रुपये बैंक ट्रांसफर से दे दूंगा।` | ₹2,000 / 2026-11-16 / BANK | Same | Yes |

The second miss is an ASR substitution, not a parser failure: the parser correctly extracted the wrong `3500` in the transcript. A live borrower hearing “₹3,500” could reject it; this demo does not claim they would. Repeated Prisma calls on identical synthetic audio returned different borderline numbers in earlier runs (`325`, `350`, `3500`), so this small score is not stable. No noise was added; the optional noisy evaluation was not run.
